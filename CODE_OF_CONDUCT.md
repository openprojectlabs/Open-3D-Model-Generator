# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be welcoming, respectful and useful to everyone.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while addressing problems clearly.
- Welcome different experience levels and viewpoints.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and deliberate disruption.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues or restrict participation when behavior violates these standards.

For sensitive concerns, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's listed contact method.
